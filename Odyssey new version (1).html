Google Sheet link

Paste a Google Sheet link to import participant details and embedded passport photos together. The sheet must allow anyone with the link to view it.

For automatic Google Sheets import, open Start Odyssey.cmd from the same folder, then paste your link in the window it opens.

CSV file

Export your Google Sheet as File → Download → CSV, then upload it here.

PDF export

Upload a PDF export of your sheet. Text is pulled out automatically and split into rows/columns — this is a best-effort read, so double-check it on the next screen.

Paste CSV directly

Copy cells from your sheet (or paste raw CSV text) straight in here.

Review and fix your rows

Every cell is editable — click a cell to correct anything the import got wrong. Use the buttons to add or remove rows and columns.

Attach passport photos

Google Sheet imports attach embedded passport photos automatically. For CSV or PDF imports, or to replace a photo, upload files here and match their filenames to participant names. You can also attach photos individually below.
